# Batch 43 — electronics atlas

Generated 2026-10-03 as one 3×3 atlas, then split row-major and normalized with
`prepare_candidate.py`. The generator source is preserved at
`raw/batch-43-atlas.png`.

Style: pure solid white glyphs on transparency; austere, serious and
post-apocalyptic; bold outer contours; at most 1–3 broad cutouts; readable at
16–32 px; no labels, scenery, shading or fine texture.

Row-major subjects:

1. Detektor ruchu — compact handheld sensor, large radar screen, short antenna.
2. Komputer osobisty — battered tower, simple monitor, one cable.
3. Komputer gamingowy — heavy tower, three broad fan vents, monitor.
4. Laptop — open rugged laptop with broad screen and solid keyboard deck.
5. Kontroler zdalnego sterowania — two large sticks and short antenna.
6. Miernik skażenia chemicznego — large gauge and thick cabled probe.
7. Odtwarzacz CD — round portable player and headphones.
8. Wykrywacz metalu — diagonal shaft, round coil, compact control box.
9. Wytrychy elektroniczne — decoder box with two heavy probe leads.

The raw atlas contained low-alpha residue between cells. Per-cell normalization
removed it; the review candidates retain only the intended connected glyphs.
