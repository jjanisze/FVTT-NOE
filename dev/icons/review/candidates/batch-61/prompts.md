# Batch 61 generation prompts

All three generations use the built-in image generator. Output is one square 3×3
atlas in exact row-major order. Each cell contains one centered, isolated,
pure-white icon on solid black. The approved 5×5 sheet is the style reference.
Atlases A and B additionally receive a sheet made from the exact GM-selected
candidate for every revision in the corresponding leading cells.

Shared constraints: serious utilitarian post-apocalyptic UI glyph; broad solid
shapes; very low detail; clean hard edges; large negative-space cutouts only;
no texture, grunge, scratches, speckles, dithering, halftone, gradients, grey,
shadows, borders, labels, letters, numbers, cell dividers, anime styling, cute
expressions, decorative gears, or stray fragments. Subjects must remain readable
at 16 px. Keep every glyph comfortably inside its cell with generous margins.

## Atlas A

Revise the corresponding input cell while keeping its concept recognizable:

1. Ruch — a colossal organic alligator-like Megator with irregular armadillo
   scales effortlessly crushes a car beneath it; add three bold speed streaks.
2. Karabin laserowy — only one crude industrial laser rifle, reduced to a strong
   clean weapon silhouette with a vented emitter and boxy power cell.
3. Ćwiczenie czyni mistrza — only two stages of the same athlete: preparing to
   lift and holding the barbell overhead; retain one bold upward progress arrow.
4. Grad ołowiu — one crude rifle with one muzzle flash at the barrel and a dense
   fan of long parallel bullets and streaks; no floating muzzle flashes.
5. Ja już swoje odchorowałem — a scarred survivor’s chest behind one bold shield
   while three disease particles break against it; simplify to a few shapes.
6. Jeśli ma silnik, to ruszy — one large wrench turns one battered engine block;
   one small ignition spark; remove hoses and secondary mechanical detail.
7. Pakowanie — one hand clearly pushes a wrench into an overfilled backpack;
   one bold arrow points inward into the open pack.
8. Paszcza — enormous mutant alligator jaws restrain an adult whose arms visibly
   push against the teeth while the trapped body struggles to escape; no gore.
9. Patriota — simplified uniformed survivor torso; one hand touches a flag patch
   fixed to the left shoulder; one small additional skill token nearby.

## Atlas B

The first row revises the corresponding input cells. Rows two and three are new:

1. Urodzony morderca — one clean combat knife beside a one-pip die and one thick
   reroll arrow; repair the knife silhouette; completely clean solid fills.
2. Wychuchana spluwa — exactly two white-gloved hands: one daintily holds a
   pistol, the other wipes it with one fine cloth; remove every spare hand.
3. Zawzięty sukinkot — one grizzled wounded adult man forcefully rising from the
   ground under one thick upward arrow; simplify the body and clothing.
4. Na symulatorach to działało — two hands confidently work a battered steering
   wheel; behind it one small simulator screen repeats the wheel silhouette.
5. Telepata — one wary profile reads another person’s expression; one bold eye
   and a short mental signal connect the two faces.
6. Przycelowanie — one rifle sight crosshair locks onto the knee of a running
   human silhouette, clearly communicating a deliberate called shot.
7. Samuraj — two hands rapidly draw one slim blade from its scabbard in a strong
   diagonal sweep; practical survivor gear, no historical costume detail.
8. Nówka sztuka — one pristine new pistol rises from an opened sealed bunker
   equipment crate; factory-clean object, no text or price symbols.
9. Przedwojenna edukacja — one intact open technical manual with a simple bridge
   diagram on one page and one solid wrench laid across the other.

6. Górnik z dziada pradziada — one powerful miner carries a huge ore sack on one

9. Włóczykij — one rugged boot strides from reed marsh into a sand dune while a
