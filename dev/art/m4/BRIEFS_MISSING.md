## Vehicle tokens — what is different from creature tokens

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Rectangular frame = the vehicle's footprint**, 300 px per 1.5 m grid square (car 1.5×3 squares → 450×900) | The module gives the token a rectangular footprint and turns it in four directions (`PLAN_m4_pojazdy.md` §4.3). 300 px/square is sharp at Foundry's maximum zoom on a 100 px grid; 400 px would make the bus 3200 px long from a 1792 px source. |
| Abrams exception: 780×2100 art, 780×1680 hull viewport, width fit, anchor 50% / 40% | The long gun projects beyond the hull's 2.6×5.6-square footprint. Keep all full-frame layers registered; do not fit the entire gun into the hull rectangle. |
| **Front toward the bottom** of the frame (south), like every dnd5e token | `rotation: 0` is the art's resting orientation. |
| **Fill 94% of the long side**, centred, clear transparent margin on all four sides | A vehicle fills its footprint; the packer refuses art that touches the frame. |
| **Exhaust outlet at the listed point** (fraction of width from the left, of height from the top) | Engine smoke is emitted there. If the art needs another spot, change the point in `gen_catalog.py` and regenerate the catalog. |
| **No rider, no passengers, no mounted weapons** | Passengers are separate tokens drawn by the module (or hidden behind dots); a mounted weapon is an item that comes and goes. The Hammer's roof ring and the tank/IFV turrets are part of the body. |
| Closed roof, plus a matching roof-hidden variant for enclosed vehicles | Edit the closed token to expose an empty cabin; keep the frame, body, bumpers and wheels registered. Passenger dots use the roof view; authorised X-ray uses the interior when supplied. |

**Generating:** DALL-E's tall format (1024×1792) fits 1:2 and 1:3 vehicles; place the vehicle
vertically, front down, and leave transparent margins after editing. The packer centre-crops to the
target aspect — for the bus (1:4) and trucks (1:3) keep the vehicle inside the central vertical strip
(about 448 px wide for the bus). Then `python dev/art/m4/pack.py --asset pojazdy/<key>`.

### Token prompt template

Replace `<SUBJECT>`, `<W>`, `<H>`, `<EXHAUST>`.

```text
Create a top-down vehicle token for a tabletop battle map, seen from directly overhead.

SUBJECT
<SUBJECT>. Unoccupied: no driver, no passengers, no people, no animals, no mounted weapons.
Grounded, painterly post-apocalyptic survival art set in the United States: realistic proportions,
believable construction, worn industrial materials, dust, rust and sun-faded paint, muted natural
colours. Late-20th-century North American vehicle design. No fantasy, steampunk, spikes, skulls,
glowing parts, magic, Soviet or Eastern European vehicles. No text, lettering, numbers, licence
plates, logos, insignia, emblems or watermarks.

CAMERA
- Strict orthographic view from straight above (plan view). No perspective, no side visible, no horizon.
- The FRONT of the vehicle points toward the BOTTOM of the image. The rear is at the top.
- The roof is closed and readable; windows are dark tinted glass.
- The exhaust outlet is visible at <EXHAUST> of the frame.

FRAMING
- Portrait frame with aspect ratio <W>:<H>; the vehicle's length runs top to bottom.
- Fully TRANSPARENT background. No ground, no road, no shadow on the ground, no tyre tracks,
  no border, no ring, no base.
- The vehicle fills about 94% of the frame's height, centred, with a clear transparent margin;
  nothing touches the edges.

LIGHT
Neutral light from the upper left, a soft self-shadow on the body only. Clear dark outline so the
silhouette reads at 60 screen pixels per 1.5 metres.
```
